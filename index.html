The Story

At the beginning, there was a leap — a leap of faith, a leap into something new, a leap that questioned values, beliefs, and belonging. And all of it unfolded across cultures.

For that leap, The Lab was born — a place to experiment, to pause, to ask what if?

Think of a leap like the frog's jump: playful, daring, unexpected — yet always landing somewhere truer, somewhere that resonates more deeply.

The Leap Lab became a protected space for exploration — a space where global minds can readjust, realign, and flourish.

About the Founder

Hi, I'm Jenny.

I'm a creative brand strategist, ANIMAS-trained transformational coach, and mother.

With 20+ years' experience and an international life across Germany, the U.S., China, and Denmark, I help you navigate change with clarity, empathy, and courage at The Leap Lab.

Read the full story

Welcome to The Leap Lab — a space to gain clarity, experiment with new perspectives, and take courageous steps toward transformation. Think of it as a laboratory: a place where you can explore, expand your possibilities, and flourish in your own way.

My life — much like my work — has always revolved around growth. Family life continuously reminds me of what truly matters and grounds the work I do with others: helping people navigate change while staying true to themselves.

Professionally, I bring more than 20 years of experience as a creative brand strategist and trend & future forecaster. Over the years, I've partnered with leading global clients — among them VW, Munich Re, and, most recently at Audi — exploring questions such as: What might the world look like in 10–15 years? And how can we translate those future scenarios into meaningful, human-centred solutions? This work taught me to combine strategic foresight with practical creativity, grounding visionary thinking in tangible outcomes.

But beyond the professional journey, I deeply understand what it means to navigate change. Being half Australian and half German, I have lived a truly international life — studying for three years in the U.S., moving from Berlin to Beijing for work, and later relocating from Germany to Copenhagen for my husband's career. I've experienced the full circle of cultural transitions — the excitement and disorientation of a new culture, the complexity of reverse culture shock, and the joy and challenge of finding belonging in multiple worlds while being far from family and friends. I know what it takes to build a meaningful life abroad — one that feels both grounded and expansive.

Alongside this, I have spent over two decades immersed in personal development: meditation and yoga, yoga teacher training with Ramaswami, studies in Vedic astrology, NLP Practitioner certification, therapy, and transformational leadership programs. I am also an ANIMAS-trained transformational coach with ICF accreditation.

All of these experiences — professional, personal, and cultural — shape how I hold space today: with clarity, empathy, and courage. At The Leap Lab, this comes together in a coaching space that is both grounded and visionary, where you can explore new perspectives, navigate change with confidence, and take bold steps toward a life that feels expansive, meaningful, and truly your own.
